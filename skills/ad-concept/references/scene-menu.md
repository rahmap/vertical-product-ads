# Scene menu

Every beat in the beat sheet uses a recipe from this table (two joined with `+` where a cell says so), nothing else. Motion recipes are
defined in the production skill's `references/direction.md`; footage recipes (`ugc`/`hybrid`) in its
`references/styles.md`; layout recipes in its `references/layouts.md`. Write the recipe name exactly as
below in the beat sheet.

Cells show the recipe per style; `-` means the style cannot make that function.

| Function | motion | ugc | hybrid |
|---|---|---|---|
| Hook: problem / curiosity / POV | Product reveal (opening on the product with a question as text), Kinetic type, or Text mask | Talking hook | Talking hook |
| Hook: before/after | Hook, before/after, or Split screen | Split screen (two clips) | Hook, before/after, or Split screen |
| Hook: demo | How to use | Demo b-roll | Demo b-roll |
| Problem | Choice, or text on a Product reveal | Dialogue cut | Dialogue cut |
| Product reveal | Product reveal, or Text mask | Dialogue cut + Caption line | Footage to card |
| Music drop / energy | Music drop | - | Music drop |
| How to use / texture | How to use | Demo b-roll, or Picture-in-picture | Demo b-roll, or Picture-in-picture |
| Benefits / ingredients | Ingredients, or Callouts | Dialogue cut + Comparison list | Product card + Callouts |
| Variants / comparison | Comparison / variants, Grid, or Comparison list | Split screen | Grid, or Comparison list |
| Lifestyle / many users | Collage | Collage (clips) | Collage |
| Proof: number | Claim number | Caption line (number spoken) | Claim number |
| Proof: result | Result | Dialogue cut | Result |
| Proof: testimonial (real) | Card stack (quotes) | Dialogue cut | Dialogue cut, or Picture-in-picture |
| Offer | Choice, Claim number, or + Marquee | Caption line + Marquee | Product card + Marquee |
| CTA / end card | End card (or Full-bleed end card with a square packshot), or Phone | Talking CTA, or Phone | End card, or Phone |

## Footage recipes (names defined in the production `references/styles.md`)
| Recipe | What it is | Style |
|---|---|---|
| Talking hook | Creator speaks to camera from second 0, captions on | ugc, hybrid |
| Dialogue cut | Spoken pieces from the footage joined with hard cuts, captions on | ugc, hybrid |
| Demo b-roll | Muted footage cut-away over the spoken line | ugc, hybrid |
| Caption line | Spoken line shown as large captions, footage continues | ugc, hybrid |
| Talking CTA | Creator says the CTA; CTA pill held 3–5 s | ugc |
| Footage to card | Full-bleed footage morphs into the shape, product photo and text follow | hybrid |
| Product card | Motion shape with packshot and text between footage scenes | hybrid |

## Layout recipes (names defined in the production `references/layouts.md`)
| Recipe | What it is | Style |
|---|---|---|
| Split screen | Two frames side by side (before/after, ours vs. the usual, day 1 vs. day 7) | all |
| Grid | Three or four frames: shades, sizes, uses | all |
| Picture-in-picture | The creator in a small frame over b-roll, or the product over the creator | ugc, hybrid |
| Collage | Tilted photo frames (polaroids) landing on the beat | all |
| Card stack | Cards replacing each other: reviews, features | all |
| Editorial grid | One large and one small picture with big type (luxury) | motion, hybrid |
| Phone | A phone showing the shop page or a screen recording, CTA under it | all |
| Kinetic type | Words alone carry the beat, no picture | motion, hybrid |
| Text mask | The product or footage inside a big word | all (footage only in ugc, hybrid) |
| Callouts | Lines from the product to labels, one per feature word | all |
| Comparison list | ✓/✗ rows landing on their words; with a head it compares two products | all |
| Marquee | A running band of promo text | all |

## Not on the menu
Avatars, generated video, 3D renders, transitions that cut the shape in `motion`/`hybrid` product
scenes. If the concept needs one, change the concept. (A shop screen recording is allowed inside the
Phone recipe, as footage.)
