# Duration and platforms

## Rules
| Rule | Value |
|---|---|
| Default | 30 s |
| Allowed | 15–60 s |
| > 34 s | Warn the user and justify in `concept.md` why the story needs it |
| > 60 s | Refuse; propose a cut |
| Hook | Visible/audible within the first 3 s |
| CTA | Holds 3–5 s at the end, inside the safe zone |
| Tail | ≥ 0.5 s after the last spoken word |

The production skill's validation enforces the same limits, so a concept that breaks them fails
later anyway.

## Per platform
| Platform | Length | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| TikTok | 30 s | Shop ads: "keranjang kuning" CTA |
| Instagram Reels | 30 s; optionally a 15 s cutdown | The cutdown is its **own project** (own `concept.md`, `scenes.json`, VO/dialogue), not a trim of the 30 s render: keep hook, one benefit, proof, CTA |
| YouTube Shorts | 30 s | Link in bio / pinned comment style CTA |

All three use the same 9:16 1080×1920 frame and the safe zone x 84–930, y 250–1460 (platform UI
covers the rest).

## Sources (secondary)
Neither is a platform's own documentation; treat them as hints, not rules.
- [vidova.ai: how long should a video ad be](https://vidova.ai/blog/how-long-should-a-video-ad-be): reports
  TikTok recommending roughly 21–34 s and Meta under 15 s.
- [inbeat.agency: UGC video length benchmark](https://inbeat.agency/blog/ugc-video-length-benchmark).

That is why the default is 30 s and why anything above 34 s needs a reason.
